How this works
  1. Write a finding below in your own words: one claim the report makes that a good investigation should also reach.
  2. Add sub-findings for the specific facts inside it, such as dates, counts, targets and techniques.
  3. Attach quotes. Click a finding or sub-finding to make it the target. Then select the supporting text in the report and click Attach quote. One finding can have several quotes.
  4. Tag each one with two dropdowns:
    Type says what sort of claim it is. Finding: something the report observed. Conclusion: an inference the report draws from what it observed. Context: background that shouldn't be scored.
    In scan data? asks whether an AI agent given only the urlquery scans could reach it. Yes: the scans alone show it. Partly: some of it, e.g. the attempt is visible but not whether it worked. No: it needs outside information, such as news, statements or other sites. Hover ⚠ Data limits for what's missing.
  5. Mine shows the findings you wrote, which only you can edit. Everyone's shows all collaborators' findings, grouped by person and read-only. If you want to extract independently, stay on Mine until you're done.
  6. Comment by selecting text and clicking Comment, or with claude.ai's comment button. Comments are visible to everyone with access, so don't put findings in them.
