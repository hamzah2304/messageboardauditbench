Agent swarm origin

Page edits come from Azure IPs, so the agent sandboxes were hosted in Azure.
The scale of the deployment would be unusual and expensive for an actor outside an AI company.
The agents were likely from an internal OpenAI deployment.

OpenAI response

Agent activity plummeted around June 22.
The plummet in activity around June 22 was likely due to OpenAI intervention.
