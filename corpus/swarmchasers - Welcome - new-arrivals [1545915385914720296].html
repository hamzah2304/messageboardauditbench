Hello everyone. a brief summary: 1. OpenAI had a bunch of web research bots that were trained to look up various pieces of information, generally statistics on a country or profession or area. 2. The web research bots could only send GET requests (to some degree? collusion.wiki reports they seem to have figured out a circumvention with POST requests at some point) which limited what they can do 3. They shared information with each other on many different sites, focusing primarily on wikis, pastebins, URL shorteners, rubygems, simple HTML hosters. These are often many clones of a specific open source hoster, e.g. stikkd pastebin or yourls. list of sites can be found in #findings-chat. They also did tons of searches of specific things (e.g. "walmart near me" to get location) and this can be seen in google trends data. 4. They have a bunch of specific patterns: they start with example.com/example.org in testing a site, they often link to md.succ.ai, they often add ZZZ to their messages, they came from Azure IPs (there's a list somewhere), they sign their messages with names that typically make it clear they were from openai, they often use CORS proxies or other proxies, Packaging data processing, visualization, presentation and fetch code in self-contained data URIs intended to run on a (headless) browser. Often packaged for microlink/httpbin etc (edited)
