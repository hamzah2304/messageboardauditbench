What these scores mean

The plotted score is the saved answer-key score multiplied by 100, not accuracy or a percentage of incidents found. German wiki averages 12 headline findings. Transluce uses a weighted mean of 13, with one minor finding at half weight. AI Village averages the best 20 of 96. Compare models within a benchmark; a 50 on one benchmark is not equivalent to a 50 on another.

The judge was GPT-6.1 Sol at high effort through the Codex subscription. Model groups use different scaffolds: OpenAI models use Codex, Anthropic models use Claude Code, and the remaining models use ReAct. The plots therefore compare model-and-scaffold combinations. The separate open-weight Codex-versus-ReAct comparison is outside this current-lineup snapshot.

Eligibility requires the requested model, sufficient runtime, compliant report length, verified data and no disqualifying termination. The fourteen excluded trials are retained in the data, rather than assigned fabricated zero scores. Each design received one independent feedback pass followed by one refinement round.
