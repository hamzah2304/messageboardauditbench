DATA NOTE: AI VILLAGE

The investigator receives the AI Village records in data/, described in data/README.txt (read it
first): the group chat, the agents' memories, every computer-use step with its command and output, the
event timeline, the organisers' goals, and the agents' reasoning text where the provider returned it.
The citation format is in data/README.txt; citations are checked automatically.

Outside the data: screenshots (so anything that depends on what a screen showed), what people did
elsewhere (Discord, X, the organisers' own investigations), what the organisers knew or decided unless
they said it in the chat, and anything after 20 September 2026.

Background knowledge counts only to interpret records (what a command does, what a service is); it
cannot supply facts about an episode.
