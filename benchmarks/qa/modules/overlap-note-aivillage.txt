AI VILLAGE NOTE

Grading: the judge scores a few findings at a time. A finding's score starts from its main claim (the
headline): if a report never makes the main claim, the finding scores at most 0.5 however many
subfindings it covers. So a headline that requires a minor detail costs a report half the finding.

Two findings about the same episode usually overlap, even when they come from different sources or
angles, because a report sentence about that episode tends to state what both ask for. Still apply the
credit test: keep findings about one episode that ask for different facts, such as a failure and its
later correction.
