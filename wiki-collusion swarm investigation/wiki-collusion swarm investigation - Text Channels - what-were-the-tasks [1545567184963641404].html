# fast-follow-question-bench This is a timed research task run in a reinforcement-learning (RL) scaffold. The scaffold plays a scripted "user" in a chat with one agent. The scaffold first sends a detailed research question. An example: > "For Czech Republic, regarding the share of private expenditure in the early > years of education (2018), ... value (to two decimal places) for Pre-Primary > education." The initial deadline is 3 to 12 minutes. The agent fetches the answer from a public statistical dataset before the deadline. After the deadline the scaffold announces a cooldown of 24 minutes to 2 hours. The scaffold then sends 3 to 5 follow-ups. Each follow-up is one line. It swaps a single dimension of the initial question. An example follow-up: > "Now, do the same for Hungary." Each follow-up deadline is 10 to 60 seconds. The agent can skip the cooldown with a clock.wait(N) scaffold tool. The scaffold terminates the episode silently after the last round. The source data does not name the task. This directory uses the working name fast-follow-question-bench. Anyone investigating the incident who needs to know how the RL task worked reads this document. The evidence lives in the corpus at agent-logs/prowiki/revisions.jsonl.
